## 中文商店页草稿

**短描述**

营老爷夜巡的队伍里，多出了一顶轿。你是刚死去的阿澄，在一炷香内附身灯、锣与纸扎，往返现实与灵域，救下阿弟，也追问自己为何死在这一夜。

**长描述**

一声锣响，溪尾村的夜巡出发了。阿澄本该在队伍里抬轿，如今，他只能看着弟弟站到自己原来的位置。灯架将倒，香已经点上。

沿物件跳附，用一件件日常器物改写眼前的事。举高花灯能让人看清断处，响一声锣能让队伍停步。在灵域观察物件和路线，回到现实动手；失手时回到香点，再试另一种次序。

队尾多出来的轿里，也有东西会附身。它占住你要用的物件，又在某些地方等你应它一声。从茶铺、祠堂到桥头与西巷，顺着六章的谜题追查断绳的来由，听村里的人各自说完这一夜。

游戏以潮汕营老爷、英歌、锣鼓与纸扎为背景，采用低饱和手绘水彩呈现现实和灵域。

**建议标签**：解谜、剧情丰富、氛围、超自然、恐怖、2D、横向滚屏、手绘、单人。

## English store page draft

**Short description**

An extra palanquin has joined the village procession. As the newly dead Acheng, possess lanterns, gongs, and paper figures to save your brother and uncover how you died before the incense burns out.

**Long description**

A gong sounds, and the night procession sets out through Xiwei Village. Acheng should be carrying a palanquin. Instead, he watches his younger brother take his place beneath a lantern frame about to fall. The incense is already burning.

Move by possessing objects and use each one to change what happens next. Raise a lantern to reveal a break in a rope, or strike a gong to stop the procession. Study your routes in the spirit realm, act in the living world, and return to the incense checkpoint to try a different sequence.

Something in the extra palanquin can possess objects too. It takes the things you need and, at certain stops, waits for you to answer. Across six chapters, follow the trail of a severed rope through a teahouse, an ancestral hall, a stone bridge, and the village's west alley.

The setting draws on Chaoshan procession traditions, Yingge dance, gongs and drums, and paper effigies. Muted, hand-painted watercolor gives each realm its own atmosphere.

**Suggested tags**: Puzzle, Story Rich, Atmospheric, Supernatural, Horror, 2D, Side Scroller, Hand-drawn, Singleplayer.
